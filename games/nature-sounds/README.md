# Nature Sounds - Premium White Noise Generator

🌊 **High-quality ambient audio for relaxation, focus, and sleep**

## ✨ Overview

Nature Sounds is a premium white noise generator that creates realistic nature environments using advanced audio synthesis. Unlike simple audio loops, this application generates high-quality layered soundscapes in real-time, providing an infinite, non-repetitive ambient experience.

## 🎧 Features

### **5 Nature Environments**
- **🌊 Ocean Surf** - Realistic waves with foam and deep water ambience
- **🌧️ Rain** - Natural rainfall with droplets, splashes, and distant thunder
- **🏞️ River** - Flowing water with bubbles, rocks, and bird sounds
- **💨 Wind** - Mountain winds with gusts, leaves, and distant howls
- **⛈️ Storm** - Dramatic thunderstorm with rain, wind, and lightning

### **Advanced Audio Engine**
- **Layered Synthesis** - Each environment has 4 distinct audio layers
- **Real-time Generation** - No loops, infinite unique soundscapes
- **Quality Settings** - High/Medium/Low quality options
- **Professional Effects** - Reverb, compression, and filtering
- **Individual Layer Control** - Adjust each sound component

### **Preset Environments**
- **🏖️ Peaceful Beach** - Ocean waves with gentle wind
- **🌲 Forest Rain** - Rain in forest with wind through trees
- **🏔️ Mountain Wind** - Mountain winds with distant river
- **⚡ Thunderstorm** - Intense storm with rain and wind
- **🌊 Riverside** - Peaceful river with light breeze

### **Productivity Features**
- **⏰ Timer Functionality** - Set sessions from 1-120 minutes
- **🎚️ Volume Mixing** - Individual control of all sound layers
- **📊 Session Tracking** - Monitor usage time and patterns
- **🏆 Achievement System** - 9 badges for different milestones
